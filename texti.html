Welcome to Our Special School

We are a dedicated special education institution committed to nurturing children with diverse learning needs in a safe, structured, and compassionate environment. Our goal is to help every child grow academically, socially, emotionally, and independently

Individualized Learning Approach

Every child is unique. We follow Individualized Education Programs (IEPs) designed after careful assessment of each student’s abilities, challenges, and interests. Our multidisciplinary team collaborates to create tailored learning plans that address academic goals, life skills, social development, and therapeutic needs.

Our Mission

To empower children with special needs through individualized education, therapeutic support, and life-skill development, enabling them to achieve confidence, dignity, and meaningful participation in society.

about Us

Blossoms Special School, We have been teaching dance since 2023

For more than 2 years, Blossoms Special School has been deeply committed to empowering children with special needs through dedicated education, therapeutic support, and inclusive values. Established in 2001, our school was founded with a clear purpose—to create a safe, understanding, and structured learning environment where every child is respected for who they are and supported in reaching their fullest potential.

Over the years, we have grown not just in experience, but in understanding the diverse needs of children with developmental, learning, and communication challenges. Our long-standing journey has allowed us to refine our teaching methodologies, strengthen our therapeutic programs, and build a compassionate team of educators and professionals who truly understand special education.
